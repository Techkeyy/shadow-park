# SHADOW PARK

SHADOW PARK is a mobile-first asynchronous social knowledge park. A visitor answers deterministic trivia by walking to an explicit A or B destination, receives an authoritative correct/wrong result, feeds a persistent Shadow version of their avatar, and can continue indefinitely.

The endless core now ships a validated 360-question bank, deterministic per-player deck cycling, authoritative score and Chain progression, Shadow ranks through MASTER, Master Stars, supported avatar snapshots, and a separate House of Masters registry capped at 20 rendered historical Masters. Correct answers award Shadow Score and a pooled energy effect feeds the Personal Shadow; wrong answers reset Chain without reducing lifetime progress. The pure suite is 41/41 and authoritative sequential/concurrency integration tests pass. Final long-run mobile UAT and deployment remain open gates.

The final visual pass is intentionally conservative: a quiet nighttime garden, a peripheral question monument, two world-led answer paths, and Shadows as the social hero. The current production preview is still a local CLI realm; a second rendered first-party client remains unavailable, so no simultaneous two-rendered-client claim is made.

No World, NAME, LAND, MANA, hosting, or paid assets have been purchased. `TheShire.dcl.eth` access is organizer-granted; deployment remains intentionally paused.
